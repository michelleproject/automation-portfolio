01 Budget

Checks spending against budget limits and sends an alert when something needs attention.

n8n Google Sheets Gmail JavaScript
Overbudget alerts delivered via Gmail in 6 seconds
Problem

Spending was tracked by hand in a sheet, so overspending was only noticed at month end.

What it does

Reads new expenses from Google Sheets on a schedule, compares each category against its limit, color-codes the sheet, and emails a summary plus an alert when a limit is crossed.

Result

Overspend is flagged the same day instead of weeks later. Used weekly by an accounting supervisor and a coffee shop owner.

02 Screening

Reviews resumes against role requirements, scores each candidate, and sorts them into clear next steps.

n8n Gemini Gmail Google Sheets
Screening time reduced from 2–3 hours to 45 min
Problem

One open role brings in 100–300 resumes, and reading each one by hand took days before a shortlist existed.

What it does

Picks up resumes from Gmail, extracts skills and experience with Gemini, scores them against the job requirements, notes matches and gaps, and writes the ranked list to Google Sheets.

Result

First-round screening time reduced from 2–3 hours to 45 minutes, with candidates ranked and organized for faster review.

03 Invoices

Turns emailed invoices into organized records and separates successful, review, and error cases.

Python FastAPI n8n Gemini Sheets
Manual extraction and categorization cut to 12 min for 10 invoices
Problem

Invoices arrive by email in different layouts, and every one had to be opened and typed into a record manually.

What it does

n8n collects invoice attachments, a FastAPI service runs Gemini extraction and validation, and each invoice is routed to a success, needs-review, or error tab in Google Sheets.

Result

Most invoices are recorded without anyone touching them. Finance staff only review the exceptions.

04 Research

Searches battery research papers published in 2026, scores their relevance, summarizes key findings, and organizes the results for review.

n8n AI Research Google Sheets JavaScript
Key research findings organized in minutes for faster review
Problem

Saved papers pile up, and weeks later it's hard to remember what each one was about or why it mattered.

What it does

Searches for new battery research papers, scores each for relevance, writes a short summary of the key findings, and files everything into a Google Sheet ready to revisit.

Result

A searchable, pre-annotated reading list instead of a folder of forgotten PDFs. Used by a battery researcher in Taiwan.
