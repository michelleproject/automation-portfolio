About

A process-first approach to automation.

Broken processes are rarely a tooling problem.

They usually break because too many steps happen between people, information gets passed around manually, and the documented process no longer matches reality.

I spent my career in process-heavy work, including partner onboarding, proof-of-concept cycles, and programs with many stakeholders.

Moving into automation was not a change of direction. It was the next step.

Now I focus on finding repetitive work worth automating and turning it into workflows that people can actually use.
